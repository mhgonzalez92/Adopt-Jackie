Backstory

Jackie’s forever family will build her story with her as we know little about her past. Jackie, an (estimated) 3 year old, 50lb, spayed Staffy, is named after Jackie Robinson Park, where she was left by her previous owner in mid August, 2026.

Despite that upheaval, Jackie has been loving, playful, curious, snorty, and cuddly with all the people and dogs she meets. Jackie is always eager to make new friends and has won over the whole block in less than a week of walks!

The Perfect City Dog

Despite all her eagerness, Jackie is mellow and unbothered by storms or loud noises–the perfect city dog! Jackie travels calmly in cars and metro trains.

Great Fit For Active Homes

Jackie would be a wonderful dog for an active home or a family invested in teaching pet responsibility. Though Jackie should be monitored for overstimulation and may need intervention to calm or settle, she plays well with children, and other dogs of all sizes, including her smaller foster sister. Jackie appears to be cat friendly (after observations from one weekend in a cat’s home).

⚠️ Food & Skin Allergy Details

Jackie has food allergies that affect her skin, though the exact allergen source(s) is/are currently unknown. Jackie received several oral medications and injections and has recovered so quickly!

• Prescription Diet: Jackie requires Hill's prescription diet to avoid further allergic reactions.

• Allergy Medication: Jackie will likely require daily prescriptions (Apoquel or Zenrelia) or quarterly injections (Cytopoint) to avoid further allergic reactions.

Training Progress

*Return for regular progress updates!

Jackie’s housetraining is improving daily and she usually uses a pee pad on the rare occasion she relieves inside. These rare occasions are usually due to a change in her regular schedule. In her foster, Jackie very closely followed the 3 days, 3 weeks, and 3 months rule for rehomed dogs.

Crate Training

Jackie is crate trained. Jackie currently settles when left alone in under 20 minutes. She has a medium sized (open door) crate she comfortably settles and sleeps in of her own accord.

Mouth Control & Socialization

Jackie is learning to avoid mouthing or nipping when overly excited or aroused. Her pro-bono trainer and foster mom are confident she can continue to safely play with others in any family willing to maintain her training consistently.

Outdoor Manners & Street Safety

Jackie is learning to pay attention to her name while outdoors, she occasionally pulls but walks well with other dogs. She, like most dogs, will try to seek street trash snacks—beware chicken bones!

Handling & Touch

Jackie welcomes all physical touch. Harnessing her, giving her meds or removing items from her mouth is of no concern.
